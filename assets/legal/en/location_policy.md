# Location Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Effective:** 5 October 2026

# 1. Why it is used

Location is used to show events on the map, choose a meeting point and search for an address.

# 2. Permission

The operating system asks for the device location permission. If you refuse, map and nearby-event features may be limited. You can turn the permission off later in the device settings.

# 3. Who can see it

The place you share on an event may be shown to users who can see that event. Detailed or private meeting information is limited to people who meet the app’s access rule.

# 4. Third parties

Map images and address search run through map services. Directions may open in the map app on your device when you choose that action.
