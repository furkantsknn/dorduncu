# Age Declaration

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Effective:** 5 October 2026

Only people aged 18 or older may register for Dört.

By creating an account you declare that you are over 18. An account that does not meet the age requirement may be closed.

This declaration does not mean an identity document is checked on every registration. If you use the verification feature, those documents are reviewed separately.
