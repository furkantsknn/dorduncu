# Automatic Renewal

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Effective:** 5 October 2026

Dört. Plus is a Google Play subscription.

# 1. Renewal

The subscription may renew at the end of the selected plan period, according to the setting on your Google Play account. The renewal price is the price Google Play charges at the time of renewal.

# 2. Where to turn it off

Automatic renewal is not turned off inside Dört. Cancel it in the Google Play app or in the subscriptions section of your Play account.

# 3. Effect of cancellation

Cancellation does not by itself end the remaining use of the period already paid. The period may continue until the time reported by Google Play ends. An early cutoff or a refund depends on Google Play’s own rules.

# 4. Payment failure

If Google Play cannot collect the renewal charge, the entitlement may not be extended. Dört. does not treat a renewal as active unless Play reports it.
