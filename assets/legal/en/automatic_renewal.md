# Automatic Renewal

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Effective:** 5 October 2026

Dört. Plus is an auto-renewable subscription offered through the Apple App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android.

# 1. Renewal

Your subscription automatically renews at the end of the selected plan period unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The renewal price is the price charged by the applicable store (Apple App Store or Google Play) at the time of renewal.

# 2. Where to turn it off

Automatic renewal is not turned off inside Dört. Cancel it in iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions (App Store Account Settings) on iOS, or in the Google Play app Subscriptions section on Android.

# 3. Effect of cancellation

Cancellation does not by itself end the remaining use of the period already paid. The period may continue until the time reported by the applicable store ends. An early cutoff or a refund depends on the rules of Apple App Store or Google Play.

# 4. Payment failure

If the applicable store cannot collect the renewal charge, the entitlement may not be extended. Dört. does not treat a renewal as active unless the store reports it.
