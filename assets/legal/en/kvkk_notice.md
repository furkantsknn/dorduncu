# KVKK Privacy Notice

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Effective:** 5 October 2026

This notice meets the information duty under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698. The Privacy Policy is a separate document.

# 1. Controller

The controller is the natural person who operates the Dört. application.

Requests and general contact: iletisim@dortapp.com

Support: destek@dortapp.com

Website: https://dortapp.com

# 2. Purposes

Personal data is processed to:

- create an account and verify the phone number
- show profile and public profile information
- create, apply to and join free events
- provide event chat and support requests
- deliver notifications
- operate location and meeting-point features
- handle blocking, reports and safety review
- review verification documents
- verify Dört. Plus and digital badge purchases made through Google Play
- suspend, close or delete an account
- meet legal duties and protect security

# 3. Legal grounds

Data may be processed under Article 5 where it is necessary to perform a contract, to comply with a legal duty, to establish or protect a right, or for the controller’s legitimate interests without harming the person’s fundamental rights.

A device location permission is requested separately by the operating system. That permission does not mean every processing activity is based on consent.

# 4. Categories

- identity and profile: name, account details, photo, selected games
- contact: phone number
- location: location shared or permitted for an event or meeting point
- user content: events, applications, messages and support records
- security: session, device, login history, safety and audit logs
- verification: verification request and uploaded documents
- purchases: Google Play transaction reference, product id, and subscription or badge entitlement. The app does not store card details

# 5. Sharing

Data may be shared only as needed with:

- Google Firebase, for authentication, database, file storage, notifications, app check and server functions
- Google Play, for digital purchases and subscription verification
- map and address search services, to show or search an event location
- public authorities, where a legal duty requires it

Personal data is not sold for independent advertising. Event and chat content may be shown to other users when the feature requires it.

# 6. Retention

Account data is kept while the account is open so the service can run.

When deletion finishes, the login, profile, public profile, profile photos and verification documents are deleted. Identity fields in login and audit logs may be anonymized. A digest of the phone number may be kept to block registering again with the same number.

Content in other users’ events and messages, and limited records needed for security, disputes or legal duties, may be kept.

# 7. Rights

Under Article 11 you may ask whether data is processed, request information, learn the purpose and recipients, ask for correction or deletion, object to a result produced solely by automated analysis, and claim compensation for unlawful damage.

# 8. Requests

Send requests through these channels:

- https://dortapp.com

Include the phone number that shows the account is yours. Requests are handled within the statutory periods.
