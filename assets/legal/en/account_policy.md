# Account Policy

**Version:** 1.0.1

**Updated:** 19 October 2026

# 1. Account

The account is opened with a phone number and belongs to you. It cannot be transferred.

# 2. Suspension and closure

The account may be suspended or closed for a breach of the community rules, deception, a safety risk, a legal request, or abuse of the service. A suspended account cannot use the app.

# 3. Deletion in the app

You can request account deletion from Settings. The request is recorded, the session ends, and the account cannot be used until processing finishes. Deletion does not finish at the moment the request is saved.

When processing completes, the login, profile, public profile, profile photos and verification documents are deleted.

Deleting the Dört. account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel an active Dört. Plus subscription in Google Play subscriptions before requesting account deletion.

# 4. If you cannot open the app

Email destek@dortapp.com from a message that includes your registered phone number, with the subject “Dört. Account Deletion Request”. The email alone does not finish deletion. The request is handled through the account deletion process.

Website: https://dortapp.com

# 5. Records that may be kept

Other users’ event and message records may not be deleted. Limited records needed for security, a dispute, or a legal or financial duty may be kept. Identity fields in login and audit logs may be replaced with hash-based pseudonyms; this does not make the records fully anonymous. A digest of the phone number may be kept to block registering again with the same number.
