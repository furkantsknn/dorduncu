# Plus Subscription Terms

**Version:** 1.0.2

**Effective:** 12 October 2026

# 1. Product

Dört. Plus is an optional subscription sold through the Apple App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. It is not a physical product. Plus features are added to your account after the purchase is verified.

# 2. Price

The amount you pay is the price shown on the purchase screen by the applicable store (Apple App Store or Google Play). Payment is charged to your Apple ID account on iOS or your Google Play account on Android. Dört. does not store card details.

# 3. Period

The subscription follows the period of the plan you select. That period is the duration defined on the applicable store product. The app does not guarantee an end date that the store has not reported.

# 4. Management

Manage your subscription in App Store Account Settings on iOS or in Google Play Subscriptions on Android. Restore applies a verified entitlement from the same store account back to the app.

# 5. Membership limits

Free membership can open an event only for the current calendar day and can seek 1 player. Active Plus can open an event for any valid future date and can seek multiple players using a positive whole number. The number sought does not include the host. When Plus ends, existing events and accepted participants are not removed; free membership rules apply to new creation and change operations. Days are counted in Turkey time.

# 6. Cancellation and refunds

Cancel in App Store Account Settings on iOS (at least 24 hours before the end of the current period) or in Google Play Subscriptions on Android. Payment and refund requests follow the rules of the applicable store (Apple App Store or Google Play). Dört. does not treat a refund as complete unless the store confirms it.

Renewal is explained in the Automatic Renewal document.
