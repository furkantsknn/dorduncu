# Plus Subscription Terms

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Effective:** 5 October 2026

# 1. Product

Dört. Plus is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. It is not a physical product. Plus features are added to your account after the purchase is verified.

# 2. Price

The amount you pay is the price Google Play shows on the purchase screen. Catalog information in the app may be shown as a fallback if the Play price cannot be loaded. Google Play charges your Google Play account. Dört. does not store card details.

# 3. Period

The subscription follows the period of the plan you select. That period is the duration defined on the Google Play product. The app does not guarantee an end date that Play has not reported.

# 4. Management

Manage the subscription in the subscriptions section of your Google Play account. Restore applies a verified entitlement from the same Google Play account back to the app.

# 5. Cancellation and refunds

Cancel in Google Play subscriptions. Payment and refund requests follow Google Play rules. Dört. does not treat a refund as complete unless Play confirms it.

Renewal is explained in the Automatic Renewal document.
