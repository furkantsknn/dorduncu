# Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0.1

**Updated:** 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Dört. app uses and why. The KVKK notice is a separate document.

Support: destek@dortapp.com

KVKK and general contact: iletisim@dortapp.com

Website: https://dortapp.com

# 1. Account and profile

Registration uses a phone number. The profile stores details you enter, such as name, photo, city and selected games. The public profile shows a limited set of information to other users.

# 2. Phone verification

The phone number is used to verify the account and to manage duplicate registration with the same number. Do not share the verification code with anyone, including support.

# 3. Location

Location is used to show events and meeting points. Features that depend on location may not work if you deny the device permission. Detailed meeting information may be limited to people allowed by the app’s access rule.

# 4. Events and applications

Creating and joining events is free. The event name, time, game, capacity and location are processed so the event can run. Applications are kept to manage capacity and attendance.

# 5. Messaging

Event chat is for participants of that event. Support requests are stored separately and can be seen by the support team.

# 6. Notifications

Notifications may be sent for events, messages, support and purchase results. You can turn the permission off in the device settings.

# 7. Blocking and reports

Blocking limits interaction with that user. Reports are kept for a safety review. A review may end in suspension or closure of an account.

# 8. Google Play purchases

Dört. Plus is a subscription. Digital badges are one-time Google Play products. The app does not store your card number. After purchase, the product id, transaction reference and entitlement are recorded on the account. Payment is taken by Google Play.

# 9. Security logs

Session, login history, device information and audit logs are kept to detect abuse and protect the account. Direct identity fields in these logs may be replaced with hash-based pseudonyms when the account is deleted; this does not make the records fully anonymous.

# 10. Deletion and retention

Request deletion from Settings. If you cannot open the app, email your registered phone number to destek@dortapp.com. An email alone does not finish deletion.

When processing completes, the login, profile, public profile, profile photos and verification documents are deleted. Content that remains in other users’ events or messages, and limited records required for security, disputes, or a legal or financial duty, may be kept. A digest of the phone number may be kept to block registering again with the same number.

Deleting the Dört. account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel it in Google Play subscriptions.

# 11. Third-party services

Google Firebase is used for the account, storage, notifications and server functions. Digital payments run through Google Play. Map display and address search use map services.

Information is not sold for independent advertising.
