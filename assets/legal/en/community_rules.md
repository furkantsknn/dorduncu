# Community Rules

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Effective:** 5 October 2026

These rules apply inside Dört.

# 1. Harassment and threats

Harassment, persistent unwanted contact and threats are prohibited.

# 2. Hate speech and inappropriate content

Hate speech, discrimination and inappropriate content are prohibited.

# 3. Fraud and spam

Fraud, misleading events, repeated invites and spam are prohibited. No one may ask you for a verification code, password or card details. Dört. support will not ask for them either.

# 4. Fake profiles and abuse

Fake profiles, someone else’s photo, using someone else’s account and abusing the system are prohibited.

# 5. Safety

Conduct that puts another user’s safety at risk is prohibited. Judge the place and the person yourself before you meet.

# 6. Reports

You can block a user who bothers you and you can report them in the app. If there is an immediate danger, contact local authorities.

Support: destek@dortapp.com
