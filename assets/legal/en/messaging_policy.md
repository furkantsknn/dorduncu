# Messaging Policy

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Effective:** 5 October 2026

# 1. Scope

Messaging is for event participants to communicate about that event. Support requests use a separate channel.

# 2. Rules

Harassment, threats, spam, fraud and illegal content are prohibited. Do not ask for or share verification codes, passwords or card details.

# 3. Visibility

Event messages are shown to users who are allowed to communicate in that event. Support messages are seen by the people handling the support process.

# 4. Blocking and reports

You can block a user who bothers you and you can report a message. Review may remove content or restrict an account.

# 5. Deletion

When your account is deleted, your profile is deleted. Messages that remain in another user’s event chat may be kept so that chat can continue.
